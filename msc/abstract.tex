Deadlocks are difficult to diagnose and distinguish from other causes of application hangs.
We propose reporting deadlocks as runtime exceptions to help developers identify them promptly and accurately.
Our bug-report study found that more than 90\% of identified deadlocks involved two threads acquiring two locks (TTTL deadlocks).
We extended Java's {\tt ReentrantLock} class to detect these deadlocks and evaluated its overhead with a conservative benchmark.
For applications whose execution time is not dominated by locking, the estimated overhead is below 6\%.
In two usability experiments, students using our approach spent 16.87\% to 30.7\% less time on tasks on average, with lock type the most significant factor in task completion time.
One experiment also showed a statistically significant improvement in accuracy, with 81.25\% more correctly identified bugs.
